Our Story

From a God-inspired idea to a brand built for excellence.

Lioness Enterprise Coconut was created around a simple belief: everyday Nigerian ingredients can become extraordinary products when they are handled with care, creativity and integrity.

We are building a modern coconut brand that brings natural goodness closer to families, students, snack lovers, bakers, food businesses and retailers. Our approach combines local sourcing, thoughtful processing, attractive presentation and customer-first service.
